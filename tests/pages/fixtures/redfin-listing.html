About this home

Presented by Lindsay Neuren
 | 

Set within highly coveted Cat Mountain in Northwest Hills, this classic brick residence blends architectural warmth with refined modern upgrades. Mature shade trees frame a charming front porch sitting area, setting an inviting tone for the interior beyond. Inside, fresh light-oak wide-plank flooring and designer tile sweep across an airy main level designed for effortless connection. Recessed lighting brightens the open layout, while main-floor interior doors have been updated throughout. The central living room—highlighted by a dramatic floor-to-ceiling limestone fireplace and a sleek black ceiling fan—unfolds seamlessly into the dining area and culinary hub. Anchored by an industrial stainless steel ventilation hood over a gas range, the gourmet kitchen boasts pristine white cabinetry with glass-front uppers, extensive full-height storage pantries, and striking granite countertops. Glass French doors open out to the back deck, blurring the line between indoor comfort and outdoor leisure. Near the entryway, a remodeled laundry/mudroom drop zone introduces smart built-in seating and storage. The primary suite serves as an exclusive retreat, showcase-ready with its own floor-to-ceiling limestone fireplace, a rustic wood mantel, plantation shutters, and direct French door access to the rear grounds. The fully remodeled primary bath offers dual wood vanities paired with matte black hardware, light-sage accent walls, a soaking tub, and a generous walk-in closet. A thoughtfully remodeled powder room serves guests on the main floor. Beyond the visible finishes, structural systems have been comprehensively upgraded—including a complete HVAC replacement (furnace and AC units), full-home ductwork replacement, a fresh garage door with automatic opener, and an installed water pressure reduction valve (PRV). Outdoors, an expansive wooden deck draped in string lights sits beneath a mature oak canopy, creating a private retreat perfect for al fresco dining. Situated in Northwest Austin, residents enjoy quick connectivity to Mopac, Downtown Austin, and The Domain's premier shopping and dining district, alongside access to top-rated Highland Park Elementary.

About Northwest
Hills
Northwest Hills, located between MoPac Expressway and Loop 360, is a hillside and residential Austin neighborhood with winding streets and Hill Country views. The neighborhood’s character concentrates around the Far West Blvd and Mesa Dr corridor, where coffee counters, local eateries, and everyday services cluster in a low-key strip-center rhythm. Mid-century ranch homes and 1970s condos sit under mature oaks, with pockets of limestone and steep grades shaping the streetscape. Bull Creek District Park frames the western edge with trailheads and creek crossings. The Contemporary Austin – Laguna Gloria adds a museum-and-gardens counterpoint to the outdoors. Alamo Drafthouse Cinema Village anchors nearby movie nights with a distinctly Austin feel.

Family Residence District

Family residence (SF-3) district is the designation for a moderate density single-family residential use and a duplex use on a lot that is a minimum of 5,750 sq ft. An SF-3 district designation may be applied to a use in an existing single-family neighborhood with moderate sized lots or to new development of family housing on lots that are 5,750 sq ft or more. A duplex use that is designated as an SF-3 district is subject to development standards that maintain single-family neighborhood characteristics.
