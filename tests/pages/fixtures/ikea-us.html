Why customers love BILLY bookcases

Since 1979, IKEA BILLY bookcases have been flying off shelves around the world. Here are just a few reasons customers love this iconic design.

Affordable storage that lasts

One of our customers’ favorite things about BILLY bookshelves is the price tag. Their affordability makes it easier to organize your home without sacrificing style, whether you’re furnishing a first apartment or refreshing your forever home.

A look all your own

BILLY bookcases come in a range of finishes and sizes, making it easy to find an option that fits your style and space. Optional doors let you choose between open and hidden storage, so you can show off your treasures or keep them neatly tucked away.

Storage that grows with you

IKEA BILLY bookcases adapt as your needs change. Have a growing collection? Adjust the shelves to fit different items over time. Moving to a larger space? Add additional units to create a larger, more customized storage solution.

Where to place a BILLY bookcase?

IKEA BILLY bookcases are designed to work throughout your home. And with narrow, low and extra-tall options, it works just as well in a studio apartment as it does in a larger home. You can use BILLY bookshelves in:

  • Living rooms to display favorite reads, houseplants and collected trinkets
  • Home offices for organizing paperwork and tech
  • Bedrooms to keep books and everyday essentials within easy reach
  • Playrooms and children's rooms for toys and children’s books
  • Dining rooms and kitchens to store cookbooks, table linens and serving items
  • Pantries for easy-to-reach spices and canned goods

Don’t be afraid to think outside the box. Line an entire wall with BILLY bookcases to create a home library or DIY built-in effect. Or use a combination of low and high bookshelves to design your own entertainment storage wall or dining room sideboard. Need help exploring the possibilities?
