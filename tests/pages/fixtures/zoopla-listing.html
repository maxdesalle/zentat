A beautifully extended four bedroom semi-detached house offering generous living space and located in Petts Wood East, just moments from the station.

Located in the heart of Petts Wood, just moments from the station, this beautifully extended four-bedroom semi-detached family home blends period charm with modern style. Thoughtfully designed and full of character, it offers generous living space ideal for family life and entertaining.

Upon entering, you’re welcomed by a bright hallway leading to a front reception room with a feature fireplace, a guest cloakroom, and a spacious utility area.

The heart of the home is the stunning open-plan kitchen, dining and family area - a seamless, light-filled space featuring a bespoke German Leicht kitchen with integrated appliances, Silestone quartz island and breakfast bar, and ample storage. Skylights and bi-fold doors flood the room with natural light and open directly onto the sunny south-facing garden and patio, perfect for alfresco dining.

Upstairs, a beautiful stained-glass window lights the staircase and landing. There are three double bedrooms, including a generous master suite with fitted wardrobes and contemporary en-suite, plus a fourth bedroom that can serve as a study. A recently renovated family bathroom with underfloor heating and high-spec Aqualisa shower completes the first floor.

Outside, the mature south-facing garden enjoys sunshine throughout the day, framed by trees and shrubs for privacy. The paved patio area is ideal for relaxing or entertaining. To the front, there is off-street parking for two to three cars and a half garage providing additional storage.

Perfectly positioned close to Petts Wood’s cafés, restaurants and shops - and only minutes from Petts Wood station offering direct trains to Victoria, London Bridge and Charing Cross. This home also enjoys access to nearby National Trust woodland for walks and outdoor leisure.
