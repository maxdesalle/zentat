American Red Cross

Mission

The American Red Cross prevents and alleviates human suffering in the face of emergencies by mobilizing the power of volunteers and the generosity of donors. To support this mission, supporters can choose from these key areas of impact: Disaster Relief: Help people affected by disasters big and small, whenever and wherever they occur across the country. Where It’s Needed Most: Support all of the urgent humanitarian needs of the American Red Cross. Your Local Chapter: Provide for local Red Cross programs and services in your community. Blood Services: Ensure that the life-saving gift of blood and blood products will be available and as safe as possible whenever and wherever it is needed.
