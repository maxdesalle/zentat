Good to know

- These dustbags filter up to 99.99% of dust particles out of the air, helping to protect your vacuum's motor
- The 8-layer material reliably catches air pollutants - perfect if you suffer from allergies

- The key-lock system makes sure they'll fit seamlessly into your vacuum, trapping dust securely in the bag
- A motor filter and a Super AirClean filter are included in the box to help improve your vacuum's performance
