    Delivery to schools

    Ordering to a school? It’s McDonald’s policy not to deliver to schools; your order will be cancelled and may not be refunded.

    Allergens

    McDonald’s prepare their food in kitchens where allergens are present and shared equipment is used. Although your meal is handled with care, we can’t ensure it’s allergen-free, even if requested ingredients are removed. While there are no nuts or peanuts in their products, we can’t guarantee they’re completely nut or peanut-free. There’s also no guarantee that your food won’t come into contact with nuts or other allergens while being delivered. For allergen info, please visit McDonald’s menu section at www.mcdonalds.co.uk/nutrition
