      Food & Drink at Clapham

      A little haven tucked away on Venn Street, our bar is a great spot for a nice drink in a relaxing atmosphere. We serve a wide and delicious selection of wines and local beers – not to mention cocktails! Alternatively, opt for our barista-made coffee or speciality tea to compliment our delicious cake selection. Our kitchen also dishes up delicious stoned baked sourdough pizzas.
