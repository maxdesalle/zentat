    About Amsterdam

    🌷Amsterdam for Backpackers & Solo Travellers: Canal Views, Club Nights & Connections

    Dreaming of sipping coffee by the canals, meeting travellers from across the globe, and dancing till sunrise? Amsterdam is one of Amsterdam’s most magnetic cities — and the kind of place where you can show up solo and leave with new friends.

    🏠 Where to Stay in Amsterdam

    Whether you're into party hostels, quiet canal-side stays, or something quirky like a floating hotel, Hostelworld.com has over 79 Hostels in Amsterdam — all reviewed by real travellers, with more than 249104 honest ratings. Here you will find:

    • - Shared dorms and private rooms
    • - Social lounges and common kitchens
    • - Free breakfasts, themed nights, and rooftop hangouts
    • - Weekly walking tours, bike rentals, and pub crawls

    Staying in a Hostel in Amsterdam isn’t just about saving money — it’s about joining a community.

    🗺️ Neighbourhoods to Explore

    • Old Centre – Classic, walkable, and packed with coffee shops, nightlife, and history
    • The Jordaan – Trendy, artsy and full of boutique cafés and galleries
    • De Pijp – Vibrant local life with street food markets and bars
    • Amsterdam-Noord – Creative energy, bold architecture, and fewer tourists

    Pro tip: areas like Plantage and Westerpark are quieter but still within easy reach of the action.

    🚲 Getting Around Amsterdam

    • From Schiphol: Train to Centraal takes ~20 mins; easy and direct.
    • Rent a bike: Amsterdam is flat, scenic, and made for cycling.
    • On foot: The city’s compact — most highlights are walking-distance.
    • Trams & buses: Reliable and handy when your legs need a break.
