Frequently asked questions

  • Why pay for email?

    Free email isn’t really free — you’re paying with your privacy. As a paid service, we only serve you, our customer. This means we have no split loyalties, no mining of your personal data, and no ads, ever.

  • How do I move to Fastmail from another provider?

    Smoothest migration you’ll ever have! We provide an import tool that walks you through it, a comprehensive migration guide, and free migration support if you need it.

  • What will my new email address be?

    If you have your own domain, your address can be anything @yourdomain. If you don’t own a domain, you can buy one with Fastmail. You can also choose from one of the free domains we offer, such as yourname@fastmail.com. Fastmail offers you hundreds of additional email addresses, so you can create new addresses at any time.

  • How will I access my mail?

     Fastmail apps are available for all of your desktop and mobile devices. Or, you can just log in at app.fastmail.com in your web browser. You can also keep using your existing email app, such as Outlook, iPhone Mail, or Thunderbird. Use one app or many, they'll all stay in sync.

  • Can I share my domain with other users on my plan?

    Yes. Everyone on your plan can make their own email addresses for any domain you have brought into Fastmail and have shared with them.

  • What if I need more storage?

    Each user comes with a generous 50 GB storage for mail, contacts, and calendars. If you need more, you can purchase up to 300 GB per user.

  • What if my needs change later?

    You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time; the new plan instantly comes into effect. A pro-rata credit for the remaining time of your previous plan will be deducted from the cost of your new plan.

  • What types of payment do you accept?

    We currently accept most major credit and debit cards, plus PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We are unable to accept cash, cheques, bank transfers, or money orders.

  • Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

    No, you can cancel your account at any time and there will be no further payments required. Please note, we do not offer refunds for unused subscriptions, so please use your free trial to make sure Fastmail is right for you.

  • What if I have more questions?

     Visit our comprehensive help center for more details. Our support team also has all the answers you need. Contact support and get a response from an email specialist.
