Zentat Options

Configure your fiat-to-ZEC conversion preferences. Changes save automatically.

Hide fiat

No original prices anywhere — not on hover, not in the popup. For when you want to stop translating and start thinking in ZEC. Copying a price still gives you the original, so you can still pay.

Your price anchors

A few things you actually buy. Zentat then shows any price as a multiple of them — “≈ 14 coffees” — on hover. Ratios stay true no matter what ZEC does, which is what makes them worth remembering.

    Price Conversion

    Convert fiat prices on webpages to ZEC. Toggle anytime with Alt+Z or from the toolbar popup. Hover a converted price to see the original.

    Nym Mixnet

    Route rate lookups through Nym's anonymous network so the rate API never sees your IP address

    The first fetch can take 30–60 seconds while connecting to the mixnet, and updates are slower than direct fetching. Requires the full (non-AMO) build on Firefox. Learn more about Nym here.
